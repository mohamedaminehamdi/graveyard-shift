Pan de muerto (abuela Rosa)

500 g flour, 100 g sugar, 3 eggs, 100 g butter, 10 g yeast, 120 ml warm milk,
zest of 1 orange, 1 tbsp orange blossom water, pinch of salt and anise.

Knead, rise 2 hours, shape the bones and the little skull on top, rise 1 hour.
Bake 35 minutes at 180 C. Brush with butter and roll in sugar.
For the ofrenda on the 1st, and for us on the 2nd.
